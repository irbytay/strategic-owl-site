Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Cosmic Serpent does not include advertising, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or a developer-operated account system. Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Optional leaderboard features are provided by Apple Game Center.

Information stored on your device

Cosmic Serpent stores gameplay information locally using Apple system storage. This may include high scores, game progress, pending leaderboard scores, and game settings.

This local information is used only to operate the game, remember your preferences, and preserve progress between sessions. It is not transmitted to the developer.

Apple Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple authenticates your Apple Account and provides Cosmic Serpent with the Game Center information needed to display your player name, submit scores, and present leaderboards. Cosmic Serpent does not receive your Apple Account password or payment information. The developer may view and moderate leaderboard entries through Apple’s Game Center administration tools to maintain fair play.

Game Center information is processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy and Game Center terms. Your use of Game Center is optional. You can manage Game Center access and settings through your Apple device settings.

Controls, audio, and haptics

Gameplay input is used to control the game and is not recorded or sent to the developer. Audio, haptic, and control preferences, where available, are stored locally on your device.

Information Cosmic Serpent does not collect

Apart from the optional Game Center information described above, the current version does not request or collect your name, email address, contacts, photographs, microphone recordings, camera data, precise location, health information, advertising identifier, browsing history, or payment-card information. It does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Data sharing and tracking

The developer does not sell personal information, share personal information for advertising, or track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. Scores are sent to Apple only when you use Game Center leaderboard features.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you delete Cosmic Serpent or erase its app data. Offloading the app may preserve its data. Device backups may also contain local game information and can be managed through your Apple device and backup settings. Game Center scores and related Game Center information are retained and managed by Apple according to Apple’s policies and your Apple Account settings.

Because Cosmic Serpent does not maintain a developer-operated user account or server database, there is no separate Cosmic Serpent cloud account to delete. For help with local data or a privacy request, contact us using the information below.

Children’s privacy

Cosmic Serpent is not designed to collect personal information from children. The game does not contain advertising, public chat, or user-generated content. Apple Game Center availability and parental controls are governed by the settings of the Apple Account and device.

Support correspondence

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your questions and provide support, and retain it as needed to resolve your request or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Cosmic Serpent’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.
