COSMIC SERPENT

Help & support

Need a hand?

Get help with Cosmic Serpent or report something that isn't working.

Contact support

Email team@thestrategicowl.com for help, feedback, or bug reports. Cosmic Serpent is developed independently by Taylor Irby under BuiltByIrbs.

Reporting a problem

Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and Cosmic Serpent app version. Describe what happened, what you expected, and the steps that trigger the problem.

A screenshot or short screen recording can help. For layout issues, mention whether the device was in portrait or landscape and, on iPhone Duo, whether it was folded or unfolded.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive personal information.

The game freezes or closes unexpectedly

Close and reopen Cosmic Serpent. Check the App Store for an update and restart your device if the problem continues. If it happens again, email support with the details above.

Contact support before deleting the app. Deleting it may remove locally stored progress and settings.

Game Center and leaderboards

If you use the optional Game Center features, check that you are signed in under Settings > Game Center and that your device has an internet connection. Reopen the game and try again.

If a score appears to be missing, include the approximate time of the run and the score in your support message. Never send your Apple Account password.

Sound and haptics

Check the game's available audio and haptic settings, your device volume, Silent Mode, and the selected audio output. If you use Bluetooth headphones or speakers, check that they are connected to the intended device.

Billing and refunds

For purchases made through the App Store, visit Apple's Report a Problem website to review purchases or request a refund. Apple handles billing and refund decisions.

Privacy questions

Read the Cosmic Serpent privacy policy for information about local game data and optional Game Center features. Send privacy questions or requests to team@thestrategicowl.com.
