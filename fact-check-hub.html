The Law That Makes Fraud Provable

Passed in 2002, the Help America Vote Act requires voting systems to create a permanent paper record that can be manually audited.

Mass illegal voting or machine manipulation would have to leave evidence in voter records, paper ballots, audits, and the certified count. No evidence means no proven claim.

Heritage’s database mixes individual cases involving both parties across local, state, and federal elections; it is not a measure of presidential fraud. Sweeping claims about machines, mail ballots, noncitizen voting, or outside interference still have to match the paper records, audits, and certified count required by HAVA and state law.
