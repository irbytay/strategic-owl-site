Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Owl Radar is a news discovery and reading application operated by The Strategic Owl. This policy describes information processed by the Owl Radar app and its supporting services. We do not sell personal information, use third-party advertising networks, or use Owl Radar data to track people across other companies’ apps and websites.

At a glance

  • Radar, the chronological News Feed, source browsing, shared story cards, and links to original reporting are available without an Owl Membership.
  • Owl Membership adds saved sources, a personalized Feed, Owl Reader on supported stories, and research prompts.
  • You can browse public features without entering an email. Owl Radar creates a private app session so it can securely request its services.
  • If you enter an email to check access, the app sends it to Supabase to validate eligible Owl Access, Substack, complimentary/VIP, or administrator access.
  • Apple handles App Store billing. Owl Radar receives transaction information needed to verify, restore, and maintain a membership—not your payment-card details.
  • The current native app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information you provide

Email address for access. If you choose to check membership, Owl Radar sends the email address you enter to a protected Supabase function. The function compares it with eligible access records, including approved users and existing subscription records. After successful validation, the email is saved on your device so access can be checked again after the app is reopened. Signing out removes the saved email from that device.

Administrator sign-in. An email recognized for administrator access is not sufficient by itself to open Publisher Desk. The administrator must continue with Sign in with Apple, which supplies an identity token used to establish and verify a protected Supabase session. Apple may provide an email address or name depending on the authorization and Apple Account settings. Administrator actions can process information submitted through Publisher Desk, including story-group titles or summaries, analyses, source requests, article or source status changes, and complimentary reader names and email addresses.

Source requests and member choices. If you choose to follow or unfollow a source, Owl Radar sends the source identifier and your app session or validated membership information to Supabase so it can save that choice. If you submit a request for a source, the service processes the source name, website or feed address, and any reason you provide. Members may also use Feed filters and request a story in Owl Reader; the app sends the information needed to fulfill those requests.

Information created for app operation

Private app identifier and session. Owl Radar creates a pseudonymous Supabase user identifier, including for people who use the app without entering an email. The identifier supports service requests, saved-source preferences, and linking an Apple subscription to the app session. Access and refresh tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. This identifier is not an advertising ID and is not used for cross-app tracking.

Membership and Apple transaction data. When you purchase or restore Owl Membership, StoreKit and Apple process the transaction. Owl Radar sends Apple-signed transaction data to Supabase for server-side verification. The services process information such as product and transaction identifiers, original transaction identifier, purchase and expiration times, subscription status, revocation or refund state, and the app account token. A separate Cloudflare service verifies Apple-signed data for subscription notifications. This information is used to confirm and maintain access, restore purchases, and apply renewal, expiration, refund, or revocation changes. Apple does not provide Owl Radar with your payment-card number through this process.

News and Radar requests. When the app loads Feed or Radar, requests may include story, article, or source identifiers, selected Feed filters, and the session or membership information needed to return public or member features. Owl Radar may cache news responses on your device for offline use. You can clear saved stories and cached news from the app’s Diagnostics and storage section.

Owl Reader and research prompts. Owl Reader starts only when you tap its button. The request includes the selected article identifier, secure app session, and validated access information; Cloudflare processes the request and may retrieve the publisher’s page where permitted and available. If a publisher does not make the story available, the app provides a notice and a link to the original. The research prompt is prepared on your device and copied only when you tap Copy. Owl Radar does not automatically send that prompt to an external AI service; if you paste it into another service, that service’s privacy policy applies.

Shared story cards. When you share a story, the recipient opens a public card hosted at share.owlradar.com. The card retrieves public story details such as headline, publisher, publication time, a short preview, and preview image. It offers separate choices to open Owl Radar or the original publisher. The card does not include your email, saved sources, membership status, private account information, Owl Reader text, or research prompt. Opening the card does not create an Owl Radar profile. Cloudflare and Supabase may process ordinary request information, such as IP address, request time, and browser details, to deliver and protect the card service.

Diagnostics and local preferences. A diagnostics report that you choose to copy includes app version and build, platform, bundle identifier, connection state, and access level. It excludes your email, Apple identity token, purchase receipt, and service credentials. Appearance and reading preferences are saved locally. News responses may be cached in the app’s local cache.

How we use information

  • Provide Radar, Feed, article detail, source discovery, sharing, and Owl Reader.
  • Remember sources you choose to follow and personalize your member Feed.
  • Validate or restore membership and protect administrator tools.
  • Operate the share-card experience and link readers to original reporting.
  • Protect the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and meet legal obligations.

Source choices and the articles you request may reveal news or civic interests. We use those interactions to provide the features you select; we do not use them for third-party advertising profiles.

Service providers and external links

  • Supabase provides app authentication, databases, and server functions for membership checks, Feed and Radar data, saved-source choices, article access, and administration.
  • Cloudflare provides the Owl Reader endpoint, Apple transaction verification, and public share-card hosting.
  • Apple provides App Store distribution, StoreKit purchases and subscription status, and Sign in with Apple for administrator verification.
  • Stripe/Owl Access and Substack may be represented in existing eligibility records checked by Supabase. Owl Radar does not receive payment-card details from these checks.
  • Publishers and other websites apply their own privacy practices when you open an original article or another external link.

These providers process information to deliver the requested service, maintain security, or meet legal requirements. Their separate services are governed by their own privacy notices and applicable agreements.

Information we do not request

The current Owl Radar app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, precise location, health information, or advertising identifier. It does not include advertising networks or cross-app tracking. Owl Radar does not receive payment-card numbers from Apple through in-app purchases.

Retention, sign-out, and deletion requests

The email used for access is stored on the device until you sign out or remove the app’s data. Sign-out removes the saved email and secure session from that device and clears its news cache; it does not by itself delete server-side records, terminate an Apple transaction record, or cancel a subscription. Supabase and Cloudflare records are retained as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain access and security, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting obligations. Apple transaction and subscription records may be retained as needed to administer purchases and related obligations. Provider logs are subject to the provider’s retention settings.

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information associated with your Owl Radar profile, email team@thestrategicowl.com. Include the email used for membership, if applicable, and enough detail for us to locate the relevant record. We may retain information where required for legal, security, accounting, or transaction purposes. Owl Radar currently offers sign-out on the device; signing out is not the same as deleting server-side records.

Manage or cancel an Apple subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Subscriptions billed by another provider must be managed through that provider.

Children’s privacy

Owl Radar is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Owl Radar’s features or information practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest version. We will provide notice of material changes through an appropriate app or website notice.

Contact
