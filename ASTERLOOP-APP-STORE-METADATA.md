## Suggested App Review Notes

Asterloop is a one-time-purchase, single-player endless arcade game for iPhone and iPad. It does not require an Asterloop account, subscription, or in-app purchase.

The player steers a luminous orb through a continuously generated cosmic course. Touch controls use two screen regions. Standard places Fire on the left and steering on the right. Mirrored swaps the sides. Compatible game controllers are optional and are not required to review the complete game.

Explorer, Voyager, and Nova are difficulty modes. The game stores progress and preferences locally. Apple Game Center is used only for the three global high-score leaderboards. If Game Center is unavailable, the game remains fully playable and retains the pending best score locally for a later submission.

Black holes are gameplay hazards. Destroying one with ten ammunition activates a ten-second double-point Wormhole Run. There is no gambling, loot box, chat, advertising, user-generated content, or external purchase flow.

The Privacy and Support links are available from the Command Deck. No demo account is required.

External service: Apple Game Center for authentication and leaderboards.  
Third-party audio rights are documented in `AUDIO_LICENSES.md`.

Before submission, append the physical device models and OS versions used for final testing.
