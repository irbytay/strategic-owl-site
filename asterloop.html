The run

Simple to control.
Hard to leave.

Asterloop begins with one luminous orb and one goal: keep moving. Steer around stars and asteroids, gather crystals, collect ammunition, and fire into the hazards directly ahead.

The universe accelerates as your score grows. Collapse a black hole and the course opens into a ten-second wormhole run packed with double-point gems.

Every action matters

Read the cosmos. React.

01

Move

Tap high or low to steer. The orb wraps smoothly through the upper and lower edges.

02

Collect

Capture crystals for points, energy cells for ammunition, and rare hearts for another chance.

03

Fire

Target the closest hazard in your forward path. Save enough light to collapse a black hole.

04

Survive

Every run is endless. Speed and obstacle complexity rise until all three hearts are gone.
